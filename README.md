## 🌟 Key Game Features

### 1. 👑 Spawn Background System
- **Nepo Baby (Rich Kid)**: Starts with ₦850,000 in bank, iPhone 16 Pro Max, luxury off-campus flat (Tanke Oke / Lekki phase 1), high starting Clout, but faces constant pressure and fake friends begging for money.
- **Trench Grinder (Lapo Hustler)**: Starts with ₦4,500, cracked Android phone, squats with 6 people in hostel, must do campus hustles (POS agent, assignment writing, thrifting, crypto) to pay handouts and survive.
- **The Scholar (Jambite Genius)**: Starts with 4.88 CGPA, library rat, study group leader, must balance mental health with relentless lecture politics.

### 2. 🏛️ Campuses & Authentic Locations
- **University of Ilorin (UNILORIN — "Better by Far")**:
  - **Tanke Junction**: Keke queues, student food joints, Tanke hold-up.
  - **PS Walkway & Park**: The legendary trek, dress code marshalls.
  - **CBT Centre**: 8 AM exam tension, fingerprint scanners, prayers.
  - **Sanrab Hostel Zone**: Generator noise, late night noodles, student ghetto.
  - **Unilorin Dam & Zoo**: Chill spot, couple dates, refreshing breeze.
- **University of Lagos (UNILAG — "In Deed and In Truth")**:
  - **New Hall & Moremi**: Heart of campus fashion, nightlife, Indomie joints.
  - **Lagoon Front**: Romantic sea breeze, deep talks, study groups.
  - **Akoka Gate**: Cab rush, yellow buses, security checks.
  - **CITS & Library**: Free AC, silent study, laptop charging spots.
  - **Amphitheatre**: SUG manifestos, fellowship praise nights, campus concerts.

### 3. 📱 Virtual In-Game Smartphone (CampusPhone 16)
- **💳 PalmPay / Kuda Bank**: Check balance, transfer funds, take emergency student loans.
- **🍲 ChowCampus**: Order hot Amala with Gbegiri & Ewedu, Jollof rice, hostel garri, and energy drinks.
- **🐦 Chitter (Campus Twitter & Gossip)**: Read viral campus trends, post your own tweets to gain or lose Clout!
- **💼 Campus Hustles**: Pick gigs: Assignment writer, POS agent, Okrika cloth seller, Crypto futures scalper.
- **🎓 Student Portal**: View CGPA, standing (First Class, 2:1, 2:2, Probation), register semester courses.

### 4. ⏰ Real-Time Day & Night Engine
- Live clock synchronizes with dynamic lighting:
  - **Morning (07:00 - 12:00)**: 8 AM Lectures, rush hour.
  - **Afternoon (12:00 - 17:00)**: Cafeteria rush, practicals, library.
  - **Evening (17:00 - 21:00)**: Hostel gist, sports, campus fellowship.
  - **Night (21:00 - 05:00)**: Parties, TDB (Till Day Break reading), sleep to recharge.

### 5. 🚨 100+ Authentic Nigerian Random Encounters
- Dress code marshalls stopping you on Walkway.
- Hostel porter surprise search for boiling rings & hotplates.
- Surprise continuous assessment tests.
- Roommate eating chicken from the soup pot.
- Benz boy compound mate blessing everyone with cash.
- Tanke traffic gridlock on exam morning.
